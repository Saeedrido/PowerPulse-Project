About the project

What is PowerPulse?

PowerPulse lets anyone remotely know whether electricity is currently available at a registered location — a home, a pharmacy, a shop, or a clinic. A small IoT device detects power changes and reports them to the backend, which stores every ON/OFF event and shows the live status on a web dashboard.

The problem is everywhere: utility power is unpredictable, and by the time you find out your shop has been without power, stock may already be spoiled. PowerPulse turns the unknown into a constant you can watch, wherever you are.

  • ✓Live power status for each monitored location
  • ✓Automatic alerts the moment power goes off or returns
  • ✓A full history timeline of every power event
  • ✓Monitor multiple locations from a single dashboard
  • ✓Works with the IoT simulator today, real hardware later
